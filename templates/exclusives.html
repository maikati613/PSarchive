PlayStation Exclusive Games

PlayStation has created some of the most memorable gaming experiences through powerful storytelling, unique worlds, and iconic characters. These games represent some of the biggest titles from the PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5 generations.

Ghost of Tsushima

Ghost of Tsushima follows Jin Sakai, a samurai defending the island of Tsushima during the first Mongol invasion of Japan. After losing many of his fellow warriors, Jin begins to question the traditional samurai code and develops new fighting techniques to protect his home. The game combines cinematic storytelling, exploration, sword combat, and a beautiful open world inspired by Japanese history.

God of War Ragnarök

God of War Ragnarök continues the story of Kratos and his son Atreus as they face the approaching events of Ragnarök. Together they travel across the Nine Realms while facing powerful enemies, exploring Norse mythology, and discovering the truth about their destiny. The game focuses on family, change, and the consequences of past actions.

Gran Turismo 7

Gran Turismo 7 celebrates the history and passion of automobiles. Players collect legendary cars, compete on realistic tracks, customize vehicles, and experience one of the most detailed racing simulations available. The game continues the long-running legacy of the Gran Turismo series.

Horizon Forbidden West

Horizon Forbidden West follows Aloy as she travels into unknown territories to discover the cause of a mysterious threat that is destroying the planet. The game expands the world with new machines, underwater areas, tribes, and secrets about humanity's forgotten past.

The Last of Us Part II

The Last of Us Part II continues Ellie's story in a dangerous post-apocalyptic world. The game explores themes of revenge, loss, survival, and the effects of violence through a deeply emotional character-driven story.

Marvel's Spider-Man 2

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 follows Peter Parker and Miles Morales as they protect New York City from new threats. The game introduces new abilities, faster movement, and enemies such as Kraven the Hunter and Venom while exploring the challenges of being Spider-Man.
